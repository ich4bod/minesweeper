Left-click to reveal. Right-click to flag.

How to play
  • Left-click a covered square to reveal it.
  • Right-click (or long-press on touch) toggles a flag.
  • Left-click a revealed number whose flags already match it to clear the squares around it.
  • A number counts the mines in the eight neighbouring squares.
  • Your first click is always safe — it opens a region.
  • Reveal every square that is not a mine to win.
